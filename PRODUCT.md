# Watchify

Independent B2C watch buying and selling experience. No customer-facing Talia identity. Existing logo and the sample account Joao Almeida stay. The current deliverable is a local source ZIP; no repository publication or deployment.

People should be able to explore real reference photographs, filter watches, compare and save a shortlist, inspect condition/accessories, and create a seller listing. Selling includes photo upload, optional configured private image assessment and market pricing, and explicit polishing, wear and condition declarations. A photograph cannot certify authenticity or hidden mechanical condition. Fixtures remain fixtures; existing live integrations are optional and must not be described as connected unless they are.

Design goal: distinctive, high-end, fluid and readable. The previous cream editorial layout was rejected. Replace the visual world while preserving working flows. Treat buying, selling and account as equal product areas. English UI, Portuguese project communication.
